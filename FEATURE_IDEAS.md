# Feature ideas

Potential follow-up features for vscribe:

- **Transcript editing** — correct recognition errors and save the edited text.
- **Transcript search** — find words or phrases and jump to matching timestamps.
- **Improved history** — filter and sort past jobs, rename results, and manage saved transcripts.
- **Speaker labels** — distinguish speakers in interviews and meetings.
- **Summaries and action items** — generate concise notes from a transcript; requires a separate text-generation service.
- **Additional export formats** — offer formats such as DOCX or PDF alongside text and subtitle files.
